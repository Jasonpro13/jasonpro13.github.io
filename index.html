Queer Nerds York

Looking for queer friends in York?

We’re a small, informal group for LGBTQ+ adults in York. We meet roughly once a month for coffee and a chat, usually on a weekday evening at City Screen Picturehouse Café. We're particularly aimed at people in their late 20s and 30s with broadly nerdy interests.

Coming along for the first time?

We’re usually a small group of around 6 to 10 people, and newcomers are always welcome.

When you arrive, look for the little stuffed rabbit on our table, that's how you'll know you've found us!

Who’s organising this?

Hi, I'm Rhys! I'm 35 and have lived in York for 15 years. I started these meetups in January 2026 because I wanted to make more queer friends locally and felt there weren't many casual queer social groups outside the universities. My interests are science fiction, architecture, and I edit Wikipedia!
