Queer Nerds York

Looking for queer friends in York?

We’re a small, informal group for LGBTQ+ adults in York. We meet roughly once a month for coffee and a chat, usually on a weekday evening at City Screen Picturehouse Café. We're particularly aimed at people in their late 20s to 30s who have similar nerdy interests.
